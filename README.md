One page that sums up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026. A newspaper-style front page names the day in its masthead and tells the day's six biggest stories in labelled panels: Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meeting in Shenzhen ahead of a first three-way summit with Donald Trump; Iran striking US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain after an American strike on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz; a Ukrainian attack collapsing part of the Crimea Bridge; the United States losing its measles-elimination status while Ebola grips DR Congo; Mary Peltola's Alaska win flipping the US Senate to the Democrats, 56-44; and Nvidia beating forecasts with $115.8 billion in revenue. A footer ticker adds the Siberia plane crash, Israel's new Knesset speaker and Cambridge Dictionary's word of the year. The headlines were cross-checked against several of the day's news roundups before the page was drawn. `draw.py` renders the page with PIL; `main.py` is the submitted program: standard library only, it checks the PNG against the contest limits and copies it to the output directory, byte for byte, every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# writes out/entry.png, the 1024x1024 front page
```
